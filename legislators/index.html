Michigan Legislators

Michigan's Legislature is a single chamber. All 16 legislators sit together, debate together, and vote together on every bill. Find yours by name, district, or committee.

One chamber

Unlike most states, Michigan has no separate upper and lower house. Every bill gets one floor vote, and every legislator votes on all of them.
