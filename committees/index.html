Committees

The Legislature works through four standing committees and two special committees. Bills are referred to committee before they reach the floor.

How assignments work

  • The Speaker proposes a committee roster at the start of each term, and the Legislature approves it by majority vote.
  • Every legislator serves on exactly two standing committees.
  • Special committees are voluntary, need at least three members, and do not count toward the two standing seats.
  • A legislator may be removed from a committee only by the Speaker with the Legislature's approval.
